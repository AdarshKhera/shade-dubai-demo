Notes

Shade comes from buildings only. Trees, awnings and clouds are not modelled. Heights are real where known, guessed elsewhere. Only some districts are detailed; the rest are rough.

Independent concept. Not affiliated with any government entity. Data: OpenStreetMap contributors, Overture Maps, Open-Meteo.
